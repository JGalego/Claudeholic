# Department lore bible

This document keeps the fictional bureaucracy internally consistent. It is not
an invitation to explain every joke on the public page.

## Institutional map

### Department of Prompt Health

The publisher of **claudeholic.me**. It is calm, procedural, underfunded, and
mildly concerned. It never claims real medical authority. Its seal is marked
`DPH`, Case 001, and “Est. too late.”

### Bureau of Context Hygiene

The Department's operational branch. It administers the Prompt Withdrawal
Index, maintains the case file, and certifies inspectors. It believes most
problems can be addressed with a form, a primary source, and a glass of water.

### International Claudeholics Association

An independent fictional peer group, not a government body. It publishes the
seven-step recovery program and insists that useful tools need not be abandoned
to be used intentionally.

### Ministry of Token Conservation

A separate, older institution with a permanently redacted budget. It appears
only in deeper terminal records. The Department considers its jurisdiction
“adjacent and computationally expensive.”

### Certified Context Window Inspectors

Field staff within the Bureau of Context Hygiene. They issue case numbers,
inspect prompts of unreasonable length, and are not authorized to provide
medical advice.

## Publications and instruments

- **DSM-AI:** A fictional and explicitly unreviewed diagnostic manual.
- **Prompt Withdrawal Index:** A twelve-point satirical count of selected
  symptoms. It is never described as clinically valid.
- **Annual Context Window Census:** A recurring report that is always delayed
  because respondents keep adding context.
- **Form DPH-12:** The public dependency checklist, revised whenever a new model
  drops.

## Voice rules

- Institutions speak with dry bureaucratic confidence about trivial matters.
- Sincere safety and wellbeing guidance is plain, specific, and unhidden.
- The Department can be incompetent; the visitor is never stupid.
- Claude is a useful tool, not a villain, deity, patient, or romantic partner.
- “Diagnosis,” “clinical,” and “recovery” language must remain visibly satirical
  and near a non-medical disclaimer.
- Real distress is never a punchline.

## Case numbering

The website is Case File 001. New public pages may receive case numbers only if
they represent a distinct exhibit or investigation. Build artifacts, issue
templates, and changelogs do not receive case numbers, despite lobbying.

## Sister-project continuity

Related projects are recurring characters, not sponsors:

- **vibecode** appears in the hidden terminal dependency tree.
- **works-on-my-machine** appears as an unreliable witness on the 404 page.
- **tech-debt** appears as an inherited obligation after all twelve symptoms.

Do not add more than these three public references without first removing one.
The universe should feel discovered, not cross-promoted.
