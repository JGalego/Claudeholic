02 / Progression

The six stages of “one more prompt.”

Advancement is neither linear nor mandatory. Most people move freely between stages depending on deadlines and available snacks.

  1. Stage 0

    Healthy curiosity

    You ask a useful question. You verify the answer. You leave.

    Clinical note: Astonishing form.

  2. Stage 1

    The polite return

    You come back tomorrow. It remembers nothing. You forgive it.

    Clinical note: Normal tool use with early anthropomorphism.

  3. Stage 3

    Prompt engineer

    Your question now has a role, constraints, examples, and a small constitution.

    Clinical note: The preamble exceeds the task.

  4. Stage 6

    Local MCP collector

    You connected six tools so you would not need to open a seventh tab.

    Clinical note: There are now eleven tabs.

  5. Stage 9

    Context window maximalist

    You no longer provide context. You provide an archive.

    Clinical note: Somewhere, a token counter is crying.

  6. Final stage

    Release notes recreationalist

    You know what changed before your colleagues know there was a release.

    Clinical note: You are reading this website recreationally.

03 / Recovery

A seven-step program with no login streak.

Recovery does not mean abandoning useful tools. It means noticing when the tool has quietly become the activity.

  1. 01 · Biological maintenance

    Drink water.

    The model can wait. Your kidneys have a stricter rate limit.

  2. 02 · Primary sources

    Read the documentation.

    AI can orient you. The source tells you what the source actually says.

  3. 03 · Manual mode

    Write the first draft yourself.

    Struggle is not always inefficiency. Sometimes it is the lesson arriving.

  4. 04 · Peer network

    Ask a human.

    They may disagree, remember context, or suggest lunch. All useful features.

  5. 05 · Context hygiene

    Protect private information.

    Remove secrets, personal data, and anything you would not place on a projector.

  6. 06 · Verification

    Check the confident parts twice.

    Fluency is presentation. Evidence is evidence.

  7. 07 · Carbon-based rendering

    Go outside.

    Observe a tree with no loading state. Maintain one hobby without a roadmap.

Initiate emergency analog protocol

Remain calm. Your context window cannot follow you here.

  1. Save your work.
  2. Close one browser tab.
  3. Put both feet on the floor.
  4. Look at something that was not generated.

No account cancellation is required. This is just a break.

04 / A balance manifesto

Keep the spark. Keep the skepticism.

Generative AI can be a remarkable companion to curiosity. It can help us cross unfamiliar ground, test an idea, find the shape of a question, or begin when the blank page feels heavier than it should.

But speed is not the same as understanding. A polished answer is not necessarily a true one. When we stop checking, practicing, and making decisions of our own, convenience begins to charge interest.

Craft lives in attention: choosing what matters, noticing what is wrong, and staying with a problem long enough to be changed by it. No tool can outsource that responsibility. Good tools give it more room.

Use AI to expand your agency, not replace it.

Keep learning the fundamentals. Keep private things private. Ask humans what they see. Make something inefficiently, just because your hands and mind deserve the practice. Let an answer surprise you, then verify it.

Curiosity brought us here. Curiosity can also lead us back into the world: to books, collaborators, difficult drafts, patient work, and ideas that are unmistakably our own.

The goal is not less wonder. It is more intention.
